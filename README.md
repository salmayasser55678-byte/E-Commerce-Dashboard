# Customer Revenue & Churn Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 1. Project Overview

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze business revenue performance, customer behavior, customer churn, product performance, and sales trends.

The project provides a comprehensive view of business performance across multiple dimensions, helping stakeholders understand revenue drivers, identify customer retention challenges, and evaluate opportunities for sustainable business growth.

## 2. Business Problem

Businesses need to understand not only how much revenue they generate, but also what drives that revenue and whether their customers remain engaged.

This project addresses several key business questions:

- How is revenue performing over time?
- Which products contribute the most to total revenue?
- How many customers are at risk of being lost?
- What are the most preferred payment methods?
- How does customer loyalty influence revenue?
- Are there differences in purchasing behavior across customer demographics?
- How are completed and cancelled orders distributed?

## 3. Business Objectives

- Monitor overall financial performance through key business metrics.
- Identify the main revenue-generating products and customer segments.
- Evaluate customer retention and churn patterns.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and payment preferences.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends and identify periods of declining performance.
- Support data-driven decisions related to customer retention and revenue growth.

## 5. Analysis & Key Findings

### Revenue Performance
- Analyzed monthly revenue trends to monitor financial performance.
- Identified a noticeable revenue decline starting in October, highlighting the need for further investigation.

### Product Performance
- Smartphones represent the highest revenue-generating product category.
- Smartwatches and Laptops follow in revenue contribution.

### Customer Behavior & Retention
- Analyzed purchase frequency and customer churn.
- Identified 8,806 churned customers, highlighting customer retention as an important business concern.
- Compared revenue generated by loyal and non-loyal customers to evaluate the financial contribution of customer loyalty.

### Payment Preferences
- Credit Card and PayPal are the leading payment methods based on revenue contribution.

### Customer Demographics
- Male customers contribute a higher share of total revenue than female customers.

### Order Status
- Compared completed and cancelled orders to monitor order performance and operational efficiency.

## 7. Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings:

1. **Customer Retention:** Investigate churn patterns and develop targeted retention strategies for customers who have stopped purchasing.
2. **Revenue Recovery:** Investigate the factors behind the revenue decline starting in October.
3. **Product Strategy:** Evaluate opportunities to increase sales of high-performing products while investigating the performance of other categories.
4. **Customer Loyalty:** Strengthen loyalty initiatives based on the revenue contribution of loyal customers.
5. **Payment Experience:** Maintain and optimize the leading payment options while monitoring customer preferences.
6. **Order Management:** Investigate cancellation patterns to identify potential operational issues.

## 8. Project Limitations & Further Analysis

Further investigation is required to validate the churn definition, identify the causes of revenue fluctuations, and determine the factors influencing customer purchasing behavior.

Additional segmentation by customer type, purchase history, and time period could provide deeper insights.
