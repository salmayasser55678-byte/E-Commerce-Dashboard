An Interactive Dashboard using Microsoft Excel that analyzes revenue performance, customer behavior, churn rate, payment methods, product contribution, and monthly trends
The goal of the project was to provide a comprehensive view of financial performance, customer engagement, churn rate, and revenue distribution across different dimensions to support strategic growth and retention decisions. 

•​Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
_​Total Revenue: $64,847,565
_​Total Customers: 12,136
_​Total Quantity Sold: 109,711 units
_​Total Orders: 20,000

•​Customer Health: Tracks Purchases Frequency (1.6) and identifies 8,806 Churned customers to support retention strategies.

•​Financial & Product Analysis:
_​Revenue Trends: Visualizes monthly revenue, showing consistent performance until a significant dip starting in October.

_Product Performance: Identifies Smartphones as the top revenue-generating product type, followed by Smartwatches and Laptops.

_Payment Preferences: Analyzes revenue by payment method, with Credit Card and Paypal being the most preferred options.

•​Demographic & Loyalty Insights:
_​Gender Breakdown: Shows that Male customers contribute significantly more to total revenue compared to female customers.

_Loyalty Impact: Compares revenue from loyal vs. non-loyal customers, highlighting the financial value of the existing customer base.

_​Order Status: Provides a clear comparison between Completed and Cancelled orders to monitor operational efficiency.

•​Tools & Skills Used:
_Microsoft Excel
_​Data cleaning & Modeling
